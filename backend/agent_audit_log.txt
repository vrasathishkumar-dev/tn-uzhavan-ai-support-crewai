Query: How do I apply for certified seed subsidy in Tamil Nadu?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1. ASSISTANT KNOWLEDGE GRAPH ANSWER]:
To apply for the **Certified Seed Subsidy** in Tamil Nadu, you can follow the details outlined below:

### Benefits
- **Financial Assistance:** You can receive a subsidy of 50% of the seed cost or ₹1,200/- per quintal, whichever is less.

### Eligibility Criteria
- The beneficiary must be a **Farmer**.
- The beneficiary must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**.
- The beneficiary should produce and supply **Foundation and Certified Class seeds** to the Department.

### Application Process
**Mode:** Offline

1. **Collect the Application Form:** Obtain the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
2. **Fill Out the Application Form:** Complete the form with the required details and attach the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
3. **Submit the Application:** 
   - **Village Level:** Submit to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.
   - **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.
   - **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

### Required Documents
- Proof of identity (e.g., Aadhaar card, Voter ID).
- Proof of land ownership or cultivation.
- Seed purchase receipt.
- Certificate of seed production and supply to the Department.
- Bank account details.
- Any other documents (if required).

### Official Portal Link
For more information, you can visit the official portal: [Certified Seed Subsidy Scheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/docsos).

Make sure to gather all necessary documents and follow the application steps carefully to ensure a smooth application process.

### Seed Subsidy for Farmers in Tamil Nadu

#### 1. Distribution of Certified Seeds of Maize

**Benefits:**
- Farmers can receive a **50% subsidy** on the cost of certified maize seeds, capped at **₹1,200 per quintal**, whichever is less.

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Must be a **Farmer**.
- Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**.
- Must be growing maize in the specified districts: 
  - Coimbatore, Dindigul, Erode, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, Namakkal, Perambalur, Virudhunagar, Theni, Villupuram, Vellore, Salem, Pudukkottai, Madurai, Sivagangai, Trichy, Tirunelveli, and Thoothukudi.
- **Preference** will be given to:
  - Small/Marginal farmers.
  - 24% flow to SC/ST farmers.
  - 20% flow to women farmers/women groups.

**Application Process:**
- **Mode:** Offline
  1. Collect the application form from the nearest **Agricultural Office**.
  2. Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
  3. Submit the form to the concerned office:
     - **Village Level:** Submit to the **Assistant Agricultural Officer**.
     - **Block Level:** Submit to the **Agricultural Officer** or **Deputy Agricultural Officer**.
     - **District Level:** Submit to the **Joint Director of Agriculture**.

**Required Documents:**
- Proof of identity (Aadhaar card, Voter ID, etc.).
- Proof of land ownership or tenancy.
- Recent photograph.
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST applicants).
- Documents proving membership in women’s groups (if applicable).
- Bank account details.
- Any other documents (if required).

**Official Portal Link:** [Seed Subsidy Scheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/docsom)

#### 2. Distribution of Gypsum-Oil Seeds

**Benefits:**
- **Financial Assistance:** 50% of the cost of material plus transport, limited to **₹750 per hectare**, whichever is less.

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Must be a **Farmer**.
- Must be a **resident of Tamil Nadu**.
- Must be engaged in **seed production**.
- Must produce and supply **Foundation and Certified Class seeds** to the Department.

**Application Process:**
- **Mode:** Offline
  1. Collect the application form from the nearest **Agricultural Office**.
  2. Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
  3. Submit the form to the concerned office:
     - **Village Level:** Submit to the **Assistant Agricultural Officer**.
     - **Block Level:** Submit to the **Agricultural Officer** or **Deputy Agricultural Officer**.
     - **District Level:** Submit to the **Joint Director of Agriculture**.

**Required Documents:**
- Identity Proof (e.g., Aadhar Card).
- Proof of Land Ownership/Lease Agreement.
- Certificate of seed production and supply to the Department (Foundation/Certified Class).
- Bank account details.
- Any other documents (if required).

**Official Portal Link:** [Gypsum-Oil Seeds Scheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/dogos)

In Tamil Nadu, farmers can benefit from a seed subsidy program that provides financial assistance for purchasing paddy seeds. Here are the key details:

1. **Seed Subsidy Amount**: Farmers are eligible for a subsidy of ₹5 per kg of paddy seeds. This subsidy is applied directly at the time of purchase at Agricultural Extension Centers, or it can cover 50% of the total cost of the seeds, whichever is lower.

   - For more details, visit the [Agritech TNAU website](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/expert_system/paddy/Schemes.html).

2. **Uzhavan Scheme**: This scheme includes various agricultural support initiatives, including seed subsidies. Farmers can check their eligibility and the specific benefits available under the Uzhavan Scheme for the year 2022-23.

   - More information can be found on the [Tamil Nadu Agriculture Department's portal](https://www.tnagrisnet.tn.gov.in/people_app/GoScheme).

3. **Additional Assistance**: The Tamil Nadu government is also promoting the Rice Fallow Pulses initiative, which offers assistance of ₹400 per acre to farmers. This is part of broader efforts to support agricultural productivity and sustainability.

   - For updates and announcements, refer to the [Common Dashboard of the Government of Tamil Nadu](https://tnagriculture.in/common_portal/Press).

Farmers in Tamil Nadu are encouraged to take advantage of these subsidies and programs to enhance their agricultural productivity.
